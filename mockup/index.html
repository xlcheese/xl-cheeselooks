What changed in this version

      
Based on your notes.

      
    
        
  • Style tab with xl-Cheeselooks and a second example style, Float: borderless, softly shaded, with a shadow tinted by the button's own color. Depth scales the highlights and shadows of either style.
    • 
        
  • Hover in / Hover out sliders on Global. Hover appears and releases at different speeds; clicks stay instant.
    • 
        
  • Panel & Menu has separate Roundness and Gradient for the panel and for its menus, each following Global until you switch them off.
    • 
        
  • Panel height stays fixed, as it would on a real desktop, since Cinnamon's Panel settings own it. Size no longer changes text size anywhere.
    • 
        
  • Nemo sidebar rows run edge to edge with no margin and still take the radius.
    • 
        
  • Window Borders tab: separate title bar colors, height, border thickness, button layout, and four button styles. Theme is planned for v1, the other three for later.
    • 
        
  • GTK 2 is gone from the plan.
